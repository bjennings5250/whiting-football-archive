# 1996 season archive sources

The 1996 page uses only the supplied archive material and independently verified game results.

- **Roster and staff:** `1996 Oiler Roster.jpg`, supplied through the project Dropbox collection. The printed sheet provides jersey number, name, height, weight, grade, head coach, and assistant coaches. It does not list positions, so position cells remain blank.
- **Season statistics:** pages 21–25 of `old stat stuff 2 of 2.pdf`, supplied through the project Dropbox collection. The handwritten pages cover team totals, quarter scoring, individual scoring, defense, rushing, passing, receiving, punting, punt returns, and kick returns.
- **Schedule and tournament:** Almanac Sports, “1996 Results” for Whiting. The 12 listed results total 248 points for and 244 points against, matching the handwritten season totals exactly.
- **Season identity:** the archive owner supplied the 7–5 record, Regional Champions designation, head coach Jeff Cain, and assistant coaches E. Hruskocy, B. Jennings, R. Shanley, and W. Greer.
- **Photographs:** `1996 Team Photo.jpg`, `holtrop_swider_1996_celebrate.png`, and the original printed roster sheet, supplied through the project Dropbox collection. The team photograph was rotated and cropped for web display; no people or historical content were altered.

## Transcription notes

- The handwritten rushing table records Tim Zubeck at 30 attempts, 192 yards, and an 8.4 average. The average does not reconcile mathematically; the page preserves the written 8.4 value.
- A crossed-out Dave Hoover receiving entry precedes the final uncrossed entry of 4 receptions for 51 yards. Only the final entry is included.
- The defensive table identifies one player only as “Price.” Because both Nick and Norm Price appear on the roster, the site does not guess which player the statistic belongs to.
- The schedule source marks four LAC Blue conference games but does not identify home and away venues. Venue labels are therefore omitted.
